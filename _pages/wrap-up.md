System offline. Data crunched. Algorithms deployed. The **International Data Science Challenge (IDSC) 2026**—themed *“Mathematics for Hope in Healthcare”*—has officially wrapped up. 

Hosted by **Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM)** in alliance with **UNAIR, UNMUL, UB (Indonesia)**, and **PERSAMA**, this fully online global showdown pushed the boundaries of AI, math, and medical data. Here is the ultimate, comprehensive debrief of the event's scale, the stats, the code, and the impact! 🌍💡

### 🌍 The "Hope" Algorithm: Impact & Feedback

The tech was impressive, but the mindset shift was the real victory. Based on the feedback from 69 respondents, the event scored a **4.42 / 5.00 for Overall Experience**. 

More importantly, **96% of respondents confirmed that the event either cemented or elevated their desire to pursue Healthcare AI** (42% significantly increased, 29% moderately increased, 25% confirmed existing interest). 

<div style="background: linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(155, 93, 229, 0.1) 0%, rgba(0, 201, 255, 0.1) 100%); border-left: 5px solid #9B5DE5; padding: 25px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 30px 0; font-size: 1.05em; font-style: italic;">
  "The most impactful part was realizing that mathematical models carry real ethical responsibility. In Glaucoma detection, a false negative means a patient loses vision unnecessarily. This pushed our team to prioritize model sensitivity and interpretability not just accuracy because in healthcare, the cost of being wrong is not a metric, it's someone's quality of life."<br>
  <div style="text-align: right; margin-top: 15px; font-weight: bold; font-style: normal; color: #9B5DE5;">— Team GlaucoNet</div>
</div>
